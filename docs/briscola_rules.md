
# Briscola Rules

## 1. Game Components

### 1.1 Deck

- 40 cards
- 4 suits:
  - coins (Denari)
  - swords (Spade)
  - cups (Coppe)
  - batons (Bastoni)
- The values on the cards:
  - one to seven
  - knave (fante)
  - knight (cavallo)
  - king (re)

### 1.2 Card Ranking & Points

| Cards, by rank | Point value |
| --- | --- |
| Ace (asso) | 11 |
| Three (tre) | 10 |
| King (re) | 4 |
| Knight (cavallo) | 3 |
| Jack (fante), also called woman (donna) | 2 |

- In total, a deck has 120 card points.

## 2. Game Setup

1. Deck is shuffled.
2. Each player is dealt three cards.
3. The next card is placed face up on the playing surface, and the remaining deck is placed face down, sometimes covering half of the up-turned card.
   - This card is the briscola, and represents the trump suit for the game.
4. The player to the right of the dealer plays first.

## 3. Gameplay

### 3.1 Playing a Trick

1. Each player plays a card in turn, until all players have played one card.
2. The winner of that trick is determined as follows:
   - If any briscola has been played, the highest valued briscola wins.
   - If no briscole have been played, the highest value of the card that the first player played wins.

### 3.2 Collecting Cards

1. Once the winner of a trick is determined, that player collects the played cards.
2. The collected cards are placed face down in a pile.

### 3.3 Drawing Cards

1. Each player draws a card from the remaining deck.
2. Drawing starts with the player who won the trick.
3. Drawing proceeds anti-clockwise.

## 4. Winning & Scoring

### 4.1 Scoring

- After all cards have been played, players calculate the total point value of cards in their own piles.

### 4.2 Winning Conditions

- To win a game, a player must accumulate more points than any other player.
- If the total points is greater than 60, the player wins.
- If both players have 60 points, the game draws.
